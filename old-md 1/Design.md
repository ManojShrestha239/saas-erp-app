# Visual & UI/UX Design System Specification

## 1. Design Language & Component Library

- **Framework:** Tailwind CSS paired with **shadcn/ui** accessible component primitives.
- **Aesthetic:** Premium UI/UX corporate luxury design for a modern inventory management web application. Focused on a clean digital product layout, ultra-sharp visual hierarchy, grid alignment, and extensive whitespace between layout sections to ensure supreme readability.

## 2. Color Palette & Theming (Light Mode)

- **Main Workspace Background:** Soft swan wing off-white (`#F5F0E9`).
- **Borders & Structural Outlines:** Crisp, clean borders styled in sapphire blue (`#325070`).
- **Left Sidebar Navigation:** Deep, solid royal blue (`#112258`) featuring minimalist line-art icons and high-contrast text.
- **Primary Administrative Actions & Badges:** Solid quicksand gold (`#E0C58F`) utilized for high-priority status badges and key action buttons like "Add New Item" and "Export Report".
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - Success / Positive Stock: Deep emerald (`#1E5128`, background `#E8F5E9`)
  - Warning / Low Stock: Muted amber (`#B45309`, background `#FEF3C7`)
  - Error / Destructive: Rich crimson (`#991B1B`, background `#FEE2E2`)

## 3. Typography & Data Grids

- **Font Family:** Inter or Geist Sans for crisp, legible tabular numbers and clean form inputs.
- **Data Tables & Cards:** Structured with clean sapphire blue (`#325070`) borders, dense padding options, sticky headers, and clear interactive row-hover states optimized for high-volume enterprise asset tracking.
